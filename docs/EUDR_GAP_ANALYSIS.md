## 1. Method, evidence and priority

`AGENTS.md` was read first. Repository findings come from inspected entities, DTOs, validators, services, endpoint registration, EF mappings, migration declarations and test source at HEAD. Documentation (ADRs 0002 and 0005–0008, [EUDR_DOMAIN](EUDR_DOMAIN.md), [CODEBASE_MAP](CODEBASE_MAP.md)) helped locate and interpret project decisions; every implementation claim below was re-checked against source. ADRs are decisions, not proof of behavior; where this report says "implemented" the cited source implements it.

**Gap status vocabulary** (assessed per row, repository observation only):

- **Implemented:** the repository covers the row's recommended direction for the pilot envelope, with named residual limits.
- **Partially addressed:** a relevant recorded-fact, retention or review capability exists, but the row's substantive gap (sufficiency, validation, legal determination, or a missing sub-requirement) remains.
- **Still open:** no relevant capability, or only an explicit pilot exclusion, exists.

A status never means a customer is legally compliant. “Implemented” describes a recorded-fact or evidence-handling capability, not a regulatory outcome. No gap is classified fully Implemented at HEAD.

The report separates:

- **Regulatory requirements:** the binding provisions identified in each matrix row, read with their amendments and application dates.
- **Repository observations:** behavior directly visible in source. Named tests are inspected expectations, not passing runtime evidence.
- **Recommendations:** proposed product/data-model responses. The legislation does not prescribe Provena's entity names, database tables, UI, or automation.
- **Unknowns:** facts or interpretations not established by this review. “Not found / not evidenced in the repository” does not exclude an external manual process, deployment-specific integration, or a capability outside this checkout.

Searches covered application/test C# and project files for risk, DDS, EORI, Information System/TRACES, producer, plot, lot, retention, deforestation and legality concepts. Negative findings (for example no risk assessment, DDS record, country classification, retention schedule or Information System connector) were cross-checked against the domain model, `AppDbContext`, service registration and the route registrations under `Endpoints/`. Uploaded files and free-text fields are not treated as structured capabilities or verified evidence of compliance.

**P0** = required for a credible operator pilot; **P1** = important for production readiness; **P2** = later enhancement. These are product priorities, not statutory rankings. The working pilot assumption is an operator handling a narrowly selected set of relevant products, with a named human responsible for due diligence and manual use of the EU Information System. Full due diligence is the conservative product default unless Article 13 eligibility is documented. This is a recommendation, not a legal conclusion about any customer. Conditional P0 items become blockers if the pilot includes the relevant role, commodity or transaction.

An external controlled process may address a P0 gap, but its owner, retained evidence, case linkage and release control must be explicit. Calling a missing capability “manual” without establishing those controls does not close the gap.

### Substantive distinctions behind the matrix

**Full risk assessment:** Article 10(2)(a)–(n) addresses country classification; forests; indigenous peoples, consultation and land claims; deforestation/degradation prevalence; reliability and validity of evidence; corruption, falsification, enforcement, human-rights, conflict and sanctions concerns; supply-chain complexity; circumvention/mixing; Commission expert-group findings; substantiated concerns and past non-compliance; other risk indicators; and complementary certification/verification information. Article 10(1), (4) requires a reasoned risk determination and at least annual review. Article 11 requires adequate mitigation where risk is not negligible, records of decisions, and applicable governance controls. A numeric country score or an accepted document does not by itself demonstrate these steps. [Articles 10–11][eudr]

**Information dossier versus statement:** Article 9's evidence dossier is broader than the Annex II DDS fields. Production dates, supplier/buyer contacts, legality evidence and assessment records must not be assumed to be identical to the external submission payload. Conversely, organization address/EORI where applicable, statement product description/quantity, all required locations, declaration and signature information must be available for DDS preparation. The report does not recommend sending the whole operational ZIP to the EU system. [Article 9; Annex II points 1–3, 5–6][eudr]

**Customs boundary:** Article 26(4) requires the relevant reference/identifier before release for free circulation or export, but expressly excludes exports by a downstream operator from that paragraph. Article 28(1)'s EU customs interface deadline is 1 December 2029; it is not a requirement that Provena implement that interface now. A customs reference, an Information System reference and a legal assessment remain different records. [Articles 26(4), 28(1)][eudr]

**Retention starts differ:** operator DDS records run five years from statement submission (Article 4(3), subject to Article 4a); Article 9 information runs five years from placing/export; downstream Article 5(3) information is retained at least five years from placing/making available/export; due-diligence documentation is retained at least five years under Article 12(5), with system-update records addressed by Article 12(2). Case creation/completion is not a universal substitute for those events. The EU system's own personal-data storage rules are not Provena's retention policy. [EUDR Articles 4(3), 4a(1), 5(4), 9(1), 12(2), (5)][eudr]; [2024/3084 Article 12(5), as amended][is-amend]

## 3. What the backend supports today

The implementation at HEAD supports a tenant-scoped shipment evidence workflow, plus an operator dossier layer that records facts and human decisions beside the unchanged legacy checklist. Nothing below is a legal-compliance, risk, release or DDS capability.

**Legacy operational workflow (lifecycle semantics unchanged).**

- **Catalogues and shipments:** `CatalogService` manages Supplier (now with an optional `PostalAddress`) and Product; `ShipmentService.Save` creates one shipment referencing one supplier and one product, one case, and six required checklist items. A shipment is no longer editable once a supplier request, evidence progress or a dossier lot exists. Lists, case filters, dashboard counts and CSV imports are present. [CatalogService][catalog-service], [ShipmentService][shipment-service], [ImportService][import-service], [CaseService][case-service]
- **Supplier evidence collection:** a hashed capability token grants access to one request/case; rotation, revocation, expiry and post-lock checks are implemented. Supplier details are one case-level country, period, quantity and notes per version. Public completion records submission, not reviewer acceptance. [SupplierRequestService][request-service]
- **Checklist review, status and completion:** requirements are accepted, rejected or marked `NotRequired` (no note required). `EudrCaseStatusService.Calculate` still derives `ReadyForExport` from the six legacy requirements only: a request has been sent and every required item is Accepted or `NotRequired`. **Case Lifecycle v2 ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-case-lifecycle-v2.md)) replaced completion:** `POST .../complete`, `CaseService.Complete`, `CompletedAtUtc` and the case-level `ExternalDdsReference` no longer exist. `ReadyForExport` is only an operational hint and is not the closing gate. `CaseLifecycleService.Close` (Owner-only) closes a case only on the latest Owner review when it is `Current` for the present dossier and has not backed a closure; the closure outcome follows from the review decision, `ReturnedForCorrection` cannot close, and `ExternalDdsCompleted` requires an unverified reference stored on the immutable closure. Completeness gates closing only indirectly, through the review preconditions (an `AcceptedAsCompleteUnderPolicy` review needs a complete dossier; the two confirmation decisions do not), and any authenticated member may still review requirements (`NotRequired` still needs no note). [CaseService.Review][case-service], [EudrCaseStatusService.CalculateOpen][status-service], [CaseLifecycleService.Close][lifecycle-service], [CaseClosurePolicy][closure-policy]
- **Files and geometry:** multiple documents per case with categories; one current case-level GeoSubmission (Polygon/MultiPolygon; Point rejected). This is structural validation. [CaseFileService][file-service], [GeoJsonValidationService][geo-service]
- **Working export:** ZIP with manifest, CSV summary, current documents, original geolocation file, the current dossier lot, `plots/{revisionId}.geojson` for pinned plot revisions, the current `completeness` and `latestReview`. It is available in every status and is explicitly a working-current, not reviewed, snapshot. The manifest `supplier` and `product` still come from the live catalogue via `CaseService.Details`. [ExportService][export-service]

**Retained evidence ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-retained-evidence-append-only-rows.md)).** Documents are withdrawn (row and bytes kept); geometry and supplier-details versions are superseded; `evidence-history` and version-file routes expose every version. Workflows, review, export and the public portal read only current evidence through `Current()`. Persistence guards reject deletion or mutation of retained rows, with Restrict foreign keys as a backstop. [CaseFileService][file-service], [RetainedEvidence][retained], [AppDbContext][db-context]

**Production provenance ([ADR 0006](adr/0006-production-provenance-plot-revisions.md), [ADR 0007](adr/0007-case-dossier-lot-attribution.md)).** Internal authenticated routes manage Producer, ProductionPlot and immutable PlotRevision (country, Point/Polygon/MultiPolygon, normalized geometry, optional declared area); producers and plots are deactivated, never deleted. A CaseDossier anchors one physical lot per case; CaseLot versions record reference, quantity and unit; LotContribution records a producer's quantity; ContributionPlot pins an exact plot revision with an optional production period. Supplier, Producer and ProductionPlot remain distinct and no supplier–producer relationship exists. Existing `GeoSubmission` data is never converted. Multi-lot cases, split/merge/processing, unit conversion and per-plot quantities are unsupported. [Producer/ProductionPlot][producer-domain], [CaseDossier][dossier-domain], [CaseDossierService][dossier-service], [ProvenanceService][provenance-service]

**Completeness facts ([ADR 0008](adr/0008-completeness-facts-and-reviewed-history.md)).** A mutable `OperatorProfile` (legal name, postal address, e-mail, establishment country, optional EORI) is a source for the immutable, versioned, case-owned `CaseMasterDataVersion`, adopted by explicit audited refresh; catalogue drift is advisory only. `CaseApplicability` records a human assessment (assessed product, activity type and date, operator role, citation, legal-basis version, reasoning, scope and flow support). `CaseRecipientStatement` records identified recipients or a `NotApplicable` basis. `CaseEvidenceLink` records that a retained document is offered for Legality, DeforestationFree, Applicability or ProductionSource, as presence only. All are written only while the case is editable. [Domain/Compliance][compliance-domain], [ApplicabilityDerivation][applicability-derivation], [CaseMasterDataService][master-data-service]

**Completeness read model.** `GET .../completeness` is a pure read model under the code-defined policy `provena-pilot-completeness` version `2` (`1` before Quantity Reconciliation v1), with explicit limitations. It reports three separate axes (`dataCoverage`, `evidenceReadiness`, `reviewFreshness`), never a combined verdict, plus advisories and a canonical dossier fingerprint (version 1, with section hashes). The pilot flow envelope supports the Operator role placing a single Coffee, Cocoa, Rubber, Soy or OilPalm commodity on the market by import or domestically; Wood, Cattle, Other, Export, non-single-commodity flows and roles other than Operator are reported as unsupported flows. A CN code is checked for format only; coordinate precision, legal date windows and EORI are not evaluated. [CompletenessEvaluator][completeness-evaluator], [CompletenessPolicy][completeness-policy], [DossierFingerprint][fingerprint]

**Immutable Owner review history.** Only an organization Owner (checked live against `OrganizationUsers`) may record a review: `AcceptedAsCompleteUnderPolicy`, `ConfirmedOutsideRegulatoryScope`, `ConfirmedUnsupportedFlow` or `ReturnedForCorrection`, with a required rationale, against the exact fingerprint and latest review the Owner loaded. A `CaseRevision` pins the immutable rows and stores a typed snapshot; `ReviewRecord` stores the decision. Freshness (`Current`/`Stale`, changed sections) is derived on read. The reviewed export is built only from the stored revision and its pinned rows, not the live catalogue. A review changes no requirement and no case status by itself, adds no release status, and is not a legal-compliance, risk or DDS decision; since Case Lifecycle v2 a `Current` review is the gate an Owner closes the case on (and one review backs at most one closure). [ReviewService][review-service], [ReviewPreconditions][review-preconditions], [ReviewFreshnessService][review-freshness], [ReviewedExportService][reviewed-export]

**Manual external DDS submission record (ADR 0011).** An Owner can record, per case, that the exact immutable review was submitted to the EU Information System outside Provena: a structured, append-only `ExternalDdsRecord` with the reference number and the verification number kept in separate fields, the externally stated submission time (not in the future and not before the review), the recording actor and time, an optional receipt-document relation, and a replacement history in which a correction supersedes the single current record without editing or deleting anything. Recording needs the latest, Current, `AcceptedAsCompleteUnderPolicy` review and an optimistic-concurrency token, and `ExternalDdsCompleted` closing now requires the record (after the stale-review and quantity checks). The verification number is returned only by the dedicated endpoints. This records a fact; it is not a submission, status, receipt of acceptance, approval or compliance claim, and there is no Information System connector, credential, polling, amendment/withdrawal/rejection lifecycle or outage workflow.

**Persistence and accountability foundation.** Tenant filters, composite tenant foreign keys, case locks, workflow audit events and application-level guards in `SaveChangesAsync(CancellationToken)` for audit, retained evidence, provenance, dossier, completeness-fact and review tables. These are useful controls, not a universal database immutability or retention guarantee: other save overloads, bulk writes and raw SQL bypass them. [AppDbContext][db-context], [AuditService][audit-service]

**Tests.** Test source covers the workflow, retained evidence, provenance and dossier models and HTTP behavior, completeness facts and the read model, review history (Owner gate, optimistic concurrency, lock order, freshness, immutability, reviewed export), migrations and the endpoint inventory. Representative files: [WorkflowTests][workflow-tests], [RetainedEvidenceTests][retained-tests], [CaseDossierTests][dossier-tests], [CompletenessReadModelTests][completeness-tests], [ReviewTests][review-tests], [EndpointInventoryTests][endpoint-tests], unit [DomainTests][unit-tests] and [CompletenessTests][completeness-unit-tests]. **No tests were executed for this documentation-only task**; they are inspected expectations, not passing runtime evidence.

## 5. Interpretation, model implications and verification limits

### Model implications: what exists and what remains

These remaining items are recommendations, not approved schema changes. Items marked as existing describe recorded-fact capabilities, not regulatory outcomes.

1. **Applicability records:** *Exists in part* (`CaseApplicability`: activity, role, scope decision, legal-basis version, reasoning; `OperatorProfile`). *Remains:* size/eligibility evidence, who confirms the assessment, representative mandates, verified transition handling. Keep the regulatory role separate from account permissions.
2. **Production provenance:** *Exists* (Producer, ProductionPlot, immutable PlotRevision, lot contributions pinned to exact revisions, production periods). *Remains:* supplier-to-producer relationships, duplicate/shared identity rules, cattle establishments, precision and area rules; do not backfill identities from arbitrary GeoJSON properties.
3. **Quantified lineage:** *Exists for one lot per case* (CaseLot versions, producer contributions, exact same-unit reconciliation, recipients as a case statement). *Also exists (ADR 0010):* shipment, supplier-confirmed, lot and contribution quantities reconciled by exact same-unit equality under the single-lot pilot assumption. *Remains:* shipment lines, split/merge/processing history, unit conversion and legal net-mass semantics, quantities per recipient.
4. **Due-diligence record:** *Exists in part* (presence-only evidence links, completeness items, Owner review rationale). *Remains:* country-classification version, path eligibility, legally reviewed criteria wording, evidence-linked findings, release sign-off and review links. *Also exists (ADR 0013, manual):* an Owner's immutable risk assessment with structured criterion findings, rationale and a conclusion, any-member mitigation records and reassessment history on the exact accepted review, gating `ExternalDdsCompleted` closing. *Also exists (ADR 0012, manual):* post-completion concerns with corrective actions, recipient notification records and an Owner resolution, kept apart from the reviewed history; they are not a risk assessment or mitigation decision (the risk layer is ADR 0013).
5. **External statement records:** *Exists (ADR 0011, manual):* a structured retained record of the operator's own external submission tied to the exact review, with reference and verification numbers kept distinct, actor, time, an optional receipt-document relation and replacement history. *Remains:* incoming DDS and simplified declarations, external status and polling, amendment/withdrawal/rejection, coverage relations, grouped DDS, an Information System connector and outage handling.
6. **Historical preservation:** *Exists* (retained evidence versions, case-owned master-data copies, immutable review snapshots and the reviewed export). *Remains:* a retention schedule and events, holds, disposal/erasure rules, document content hashing or protected storage, and a submitted-record snapshot.

A controlled single-product, single-lot pilot is now partly enforced by the model: one lot per case, and Wood, Cattle, Other, Export, non-single-commodity flows and non-Operator roles are reported as unsupported flows and cannot be accepted as complete. These exclusions are pilot boundaries recorded in code, not evidence that those paths are ready. Article 4a and downstream/trader roles are excluded only by omission and should be excluded explicitly in the pilot procedure.

### Observations that must not be overstated

- **Lifecycle and completeness stay separate, but closing is review-gated.** `ReadyForExport` still follows the six legacy requirements only and is not the closing gate; closing needs a `Current`, unused Owner review ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-case-lifecycle-v2.md)). A case can be `ReadyForExport` with no review, and a `Closed` case can report `dataCoverage: Incomplete` (an `OutsideRegulatoryScope` or `UnsupportedFlow` closure does not need the in-scope item set). The remaining open points are in G22.
- **A review is not a legal decision.** `AcceptedAsCompleteUnderPolicy` means the recorded facts satisfy the versioned Provena pilot completeness policy. It is not legal compliance, a risk assessment, a release, a deforestation-free finding or DDS readiness, and policy v1 lists its own limitations. An `Accepted` requirement is a requirement-level decision, not a dossier review.
- **Evidence links are presence only.** A link does not mean a document was reviewed, authentic, valid or sufficient.
- **Recorded is not verified.** Applicability, recipients, plot geometry and declared areas are human-entered facts. Polygon area is never computed and coordinate precision is not evaluated.
- **The HTTP workflow test still uses a text declaration and a simple polygon.** `WorkflowTests.Full_http_flow_and_organization_isolation` checks workflow/status and ZIP entries, not evidence sufficiency, land-use legality or an EU submission. [WorkflowTests][workflow-tests]
- **Closure coverage is backend-only.** `CaseLifecycleTests` and `CaseLifecycleModelTests` exercise the Owner gate, the review gate, outcome and DDS-reference rules, reopen, archive, the closed/archived write blocks, the dashboard buckets and concurrency over real HTTP and PostgreSQL; the local client suite and `scripts/smoke.py` have not been run against them. The DDS reference and verification number are stored strings that are never validated against an Information System; `ExternalDdsTests` and `ExternalDdsModelTests` cover the External DDS record over real HTTP and PostgreSQL the same way, and the local client suite has not been run against them. [CaseLifecycleTests][lifecycle-tests], [CaseLifecycleService][lifecycle-service]
- Complete absence of external due-diligence procedures, backup policies or integrations cannot be established from this repository. Their existence and adequacy remain unknown.
- The official API reference retains provisional wording about some features despite the adopted Information System amendment. Binding requirements, published technical documentation and verified deployed capability must be checked separately. No EU account, live API, credentials or submission was tested. [API overview — operational documentation][is-api]

## 6. Executive summary

**Provena now provides an operational evidence-collection and review foundation with a recorded-fact dossier layer: retained evidence, producer/plot provenance, a single-lot attribution model, operator and case master data, applicability, recipients, presence-only evidence links, a versioned completeness read model and immutable Owner reviews with a reviewed export. The repository still does not evidence a complete operator due-diligence system.** [Sections 3–4](#3-what-the-backend-supports-today)

Since the earlier baseline the largest changes are in provenance, retention and reconstruction: production sources and plots can be identified independently of the supplier (G11), evidence and decisions are retained and a reviewed record can be reproduced exactly (G27–G28), and parties, recipients and applicability are recorded per case (G01–G03, G08–G09). No gap is fully closed; 17 of 30 rows are partially addressed and 13 remain open.

The central product gap has shifted from "no records" to "records are not connected to a decision or a release". The chain **applicability → identified production sources and quantities → sufficient evidence → reasoned due-diligence decision → external statement coverage → retained, reproducible records** is now partly recorded up to the evidence stage, but sufficiency judgments, evidence-linked and legally reviewed risk criteria (only a manual human risk assessment and mitigation record exists), country classification, external statement lifecycle and coverage (only a manual external-submission record exists) and retention control are absent. Most importantly, **legacy requirements alone still determine `ReadyForExport`, but closing a case is now gated by a `Current`, unused Owner review (Case Lifecycle v2, [ADR 0009](adr/0009-case-lifecycle-v2.md)).** What remains open (G22) is waiver justification, reviewer authority and a release status. A manual EU submission boundary can remain appropriate for a pilot if the underlying record and accountable process are complete.

The amended framework requires separate treatment of operators, downstream operators/traders and qualifying micro/small primary operators. Annex I also changed in September 2026. Policy v1 narrows the pilot to the Operator role and five commodities by design, but that narrowing is a product boundary, not a legal determination. [Sections 2 and 4](#2-regulatory-baseline-and-authority)

This report makes no finding about a customer's legal compliance. Provena completeness checks and Owner reviews are not legal-compliance decisions. Recommendations require owner approval and, for applicability, evidence sufficiency and legal interpretation, competent legal/domain input.

## 7. Top 5 P0 gaps

The earlier list is re-stated for HEAD. Closed or narrowed parts are noted; none is fully closed.

1. **Applicability and product scope are recorded but not verified:** the assessment (role, activity, date, product, scope) is stored and fingerprinted, it is recorded only by an Owner with optimistic concurrency and can state `RequiresLegalReview`, but nothing verifies it: there is no verified Annex I mapping, no legal-basis catalogue, no evaluated transition handling and no representative or size evidence. **G01–G03**, with conditional **G05**.
2. **Article 9 production and transaction provenance is only partly complete:** producer/plot identity, pinned geometry, per-source periods, producer quantities, recipients and master-data addresses are now recorded for one lot per case. Remaining: cattle, wood species, precision/area/commodity rules, legal quantity semantics, multi-lot quantity aggregation, supplier–producer relationships, multi-lot flows and the recipient reference handoff. **G06–G16** (narrowed; G11 core model in place).
3. **Evidence is only partly connected to a reasoned risk decision, and not to a gated release:** legality and deforestation-free evidence is presence only; country classification and Article 13 eligibility are absent; a manual, versioned human risk assessment with mitigation records now exists and gates `ExternalDdsCompleted` closing (ADR 0013), but its catalogue wording is not legally reviewed and nothing is computed from evidence; and no release decision exists (`ReadyForExport` is still not gated). **G17–G20, G22**.
4. **DDS coverage and external release evidence are not established:** the exports carry useful inputs but are not an Annex II statement; a manual external-submission record now exists (ADR 0011), but there is no verified receipt or status, coverage or onward reference communication. **G09 (handoff), G21**, with a manual boundary permitted as a recommendation.
5. **Retention control and the submitted record are not secured:** evidence and review history are now retained append-only and a reviewed dossier is reproducible, but there is no retention schedule, retention start events, holds, disposal/erasure rules or submitted snapshot, and the legacy path and live-catalogue reads remain. **G27–G28** (narrowed), with post-completion follow-up partly covered under **G23** (concern records are retained append-only; no retention schedule applies to them either).

## 8. Suggested implementation order

Each step is a separate approved change. Completed foundation work is noted; remaining work is ordered by dependency.

1. **Decide the lifecycle relationship and pilot envelope (owner decisions first).** The closing gate is decided (a `Current` Owner review, [ADR 0009](adr/0009-case-lifecycle-v2.md)); resolve waiver grounds, reviewer authority and a release status (G22); confirm that the pilot is the Operator role with the five supported commodities and exclude Article 4a, downstream/trader, wood, cattle and export explicitly (G01–G05, G07, G16). No lifecycle change should be made silently.
2. **Done (foundation): provenance, retention of evidence, case dossier and master data, reviewed history.** G11, G27 and G28 are partially addressed; what remains is the scoped follow-up in steps 3 and 6, not a rebuild. Future schema work must follow the existing [database migration rules](DATABASE.md).
3. **Close remaining Article 9 provenance rules.** Reviewed Annex I mapping, precision/area/commodity geolocation rules, date-window decisions, legal quantity semantics and conversion, multi-lot quantity aggregation, supplier–producer relationships, and recipient quantity/reference handoff. Covers remaining G03, G10, G12–G15 and G09.
4. **Build the evidence sufficiency and due-diligence decision record.** The manual risk assessment and mitigation records of ADR 0013 exist; remaining: country-classification versioning, Article 13 qualification, legally reviewed full-risk criteria, findings linked to evidence (beyond presence), separation of duties and the annual organization-level review, plus an extended incident process beyond the manual single-case concern records of ADR 0012 (impact discovery, delivery proof, holds). Covers G17–G20 and the remainder of G23.
5. **Close the manual statement and release loop.** Reviewed Annex II data package, external receipt/coverage/reference records, submitted snapshot, and demonstrated historical retrieval for representative pilot cases. Covers G21 and the remaining part of G28.
6. **Define retention and production operations.** Retention clocks and start events, holds, disposal/erasure, backup/restore, governance review/reporting, and incident automation. Covers G27, G29 and G23 follow-up. P1 does not defer a statutory duty if that flow is already supported.
7. **Automate only after those records are stable.** Optional EU API integration, EU-format conformance tests, lifecycle/outage reconciliation (G24–G26), external analytical providers (G30) and voluntary grouping. Validate actual EU environment behavior rather than relying solely on documentation.

Each future implementation is a separate approved repository change with its own contracts, migrations where needed, tests and documentation. This analysis authorizes none of those changes.

## 9. Questions requiring owner/legal clarification

Questions already answered by implementation or recorded owner decisions are marked and kept for traceability; open parts remain.

1. **Pilot responsibility:** *Partly answered.* The implemented envelope is the Operator role (other roles are recorded as unsupported flows) and one `OperatorProfile` per organization, so one tenant represents one legal entity. *Open:* which legal entity and activities are actually in the pilot, whether authorised representatives or multiple entities per tenant are needed, and who approves the assessment (today any member records applicability; only an Owner records a review).
2. **Product and temporal envelope:** *Partly answered* by pilot policy v1 (ADR 0008 D1): Coffee, Cocoa, Rubber, Soy and OilPalm as single relevant commodities, import or domestic placing on the market; Wood, Cattle, Other and Export are unsupported. *Open:* exact Annex I entries and first transaction dates, mixed products, legacy timber, newly added December 2027 products, and who signs off tariff classifications and exceptions.
3. **Size/regime eligibility:** *Open.* Article 4a is undecided and not modelled; Article 13 simplification is not modelled and the pilot implicitly uses full due diligence without a recorded path. Who verifies establishment/size remains undecided.
4. **Provenance identity:** *Partly answered* (ADR 0002, 0006, 0007): Supplier != Producer != ProductionPlot; a plot belongs to one producer immutably; location changes create immutable revisions; sources pin exact revisions; no cross-tenant sharing; shared plots and multi-producer plots are deferred. *Open:* supplier-to-producer relationships, cooperatives, duplicate identities, changing tenure, and who may correct a source record and on what evidence.
5. **Transaction coverage:** *Partly answered* (ADR 0007): a case represents one physical lot; split, merge, processing and multi-lot are unsupported; reconciliation is exact decimal equality in the lot's unit, with no conversion or tolerance. *Open:* whether a case may later represent a period of supply or several lots, who defines legal quantity and conversion rules, how quantities aggregate across several lots (shipment/confirmed/lot/contribution reconciliation exists for one lot per case, ADR 0010), and DDS-to-product coverage.
6. **Evidence standard:** *Partly answered* (D4/D7): legality and deforestation-free evidence need at least one non-withdrawn linked current document, as presence only, with sufficiency left to human judgment. *Open:* which country/commodity-specific evidence is acceptable, who assesses authenticity, contradictions, FPIC and third-party rights, and which specialists or data providers are relied upon.
7. **Decision authority:** *Partly answered:* an Owner records the immutable dossier review (accepted as complete under the pilot policy, confirmed outside scope, confirmed unsupported flow, returned for correction), checked live against membership; no general RBAC or membership management exists. *Open:* who may approve due diligence, declare an item inapplicable or authorise release; whether separation of duties is enforced; waiver grounds and non-waivable information; and whether supplier completion or completeness (beyond the Owner review that now gates closing) should also gate closing.
8. **External submission boundary:** *Open.* Whether the operator or a mandated representative submits manually, what receipt, status and signature evidence returns, whether an API connector is required, and who owns operator credentials and verification-number access.
9. **Retention and incident handling:** *Open.* Which events start each retention clock, holds, storage, privacy, recovery and disposal rules, erasure of personal data in review snapshots, and how far new concerns after completion are investigated and communicated beyond the manual records of ADR 0012 (concerns are recorded without altering the historical decision and without reopening; impact discovery, delivery proof and holds remain open; reviews are still refused on Closed or archived cases).
10. **Claims and acceptance:** *Open.* Whether the pilot is an evidence-workflow pilot or intended to support the operator's complete due-diligence process, and what external controls and independently reviewed sample cases are required before making that claim.
