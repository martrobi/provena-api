## Context and evidence

Problem, constraints, affected boundaries; link actual files/tests and any owner direction. Distinguish observed behaviour from assumptions.

## Decision

One explicit choice. State what is deliberately undecided.

## Alternatives considered

Relevant alternatives and why they were not selected; mark reconstructed reasoning as inference.

## Consequences

Benefits, costs, security/domain implications, compatibility and operational tradeoffs.

## Implementation and verification

Source/tests proving implementation or linked plan to deliver it. Migration/rollout implications. Do not claim tests ran without results.

## Revisit trigger

What concrete change would justify revisiting this decision?
